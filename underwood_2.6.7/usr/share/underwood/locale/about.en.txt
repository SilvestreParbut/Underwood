------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT UNDERWOOD IS
------------------------------------------------------------

Underwood is a word processor that lives in the terminal.
It is not a code editor, not an office suite, and not a
plain notepad. It is built for one thing: writing.

It is designed for people who write fiction, essays, or
any kind of long-form text, and who prefer the simplicity
and speed of the command line over the weight and
distraction of graphical word processors. It runs on any
modern terminal, including modest machines such as a
Raspberry Pi or a server with no desktop, and is
comfortable both with a mouse and with keyboard alone.


------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT DOES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Writes rich text: bold, italic, underline and the four
  classic alignments.

- Organizes long documents with three heading levels
  (chapter, section, subsection) and a navigable chapter
  map for jumping between them instantly.

- Checks spelling in Spanish and English using hunspell,
  underlining doubtful words in orange and showing
  suggestions when the cursor rests on them.

- Opens and saves RTF, TXT and DOCX, and exports to PDF,
  reusing LibreOffice when needed.

- Switches language on the fly without losing your work.

- Works with a mouse and, when there is none, with the
  keyboard: selection with Shift+arrows, menus with F9,
  everything reachable from a clean terminal.


------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------

By design, Underwood does not include:

- tables,
- macros,
- embedded images,
- stylesheets,
- or page-level formatting (margins, headers, footers).

If you need any of that, other tools will serve you
better. The point here is to stay out of the way.
